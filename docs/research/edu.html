Research / SYNTERA Edu

AI for Education

  • Personalized learning
  • Learning analytics
  • AI tutors
  • Student success

Why it matters

Generative AI is changing how students learn and how institutions assess. Evidence-based frameworks can help education use it responsibly.

Key challenges

  • Responsible and fair integration of generative AI
  • Academic integrity and authentic assessment
  • Understanding online peer learning and engagement
  • Supporting career and skill development

Our approach

Design-based research, learning analytics, language technologies and recommender systems, tested with real students and educators.
