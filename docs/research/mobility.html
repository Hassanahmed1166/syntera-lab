Research / SYNTERA Mobility

Internet of Vehicles

  • V2X communication
  • Traffic prediction
  • Driver safety
  • Smart transport

Why it matters

Connected vehicles need fast, safe and secure communication with other vehicles and infrastructure to make roads safer and transport smarter.

SYNTERA Mobility is an emerging area for the lab. Interested? Get in touch.

Key challenges

  • Low-latency, safety-critical V2I/V2X messaging
  • Secure, lightweight protocols for vehicular networks
  • Consensus and resilience under high mobility

Our approach

Protocol design and simulation, secure message exchange, blockchain-based consensus and machine-learning-assisted protocol selection.
